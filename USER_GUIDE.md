## 1. System Overview & Navigation

The application is organized into **4 main tabs** at the bottom of your mobile screen (or top bar on desktop/tablet), plus a **Top Header Bar** for switching classrooms, syncing data, and managing administrative settings.

| Tab / Button | Purpose | Who Uses It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **📋 Observe** | Mark attendance (`Present` / `Absent`) and record the 7 daily observation questions for each child. | Teachers & Substitutes |
| **📊 Daily Summary** | View how many children are **Completed**, **Pending**, or **Absent** today; send WhatsApp slips or download PDFs. | Teachers & Admins |
| **👥 Students** | Add new students (`Add Child`), bulk upload via Excel/CSV, and filter students by classroom. | Admins & Teachers |
| **✨ Parent Slips** | Generate **AI-Based Progress Summaries**, download official PDF report cards, and view a child's past history. | Teachers & Admins |
| **🔄 Cloud Sync Icon** | Located in the top-right header. Pulls and pushes the latest records across all school devices. | All Staff |
| **⚙️ Settings (Gear Icon)** | Manage **Staff Directory**, **Daily Substitute Teachers**, **School Holidays**, and **Backups**. | Admins Only |

## 2. Sign-In & Role Permissions (Teacher vs. Admin)

1. Open the app and tap **"Sign in with Google"** using your authorized school email address.
2. Your account role automatically determines what you see:
   - **Class Teachers:** Automatically see only their **assigned classroom(s)** (plus any class temporarily assigned to them as a **Substitute** for today).
   - **Administrators / Principals:** Have access to **all classrooms**, the **Sub (Substitute Assignment)** button, and the **⚙️ Settings** panel.

## 3. Step-by-Step: Recording Daily Student Observations (`Observe` Tab)

### Step 3.1: Select Classroom & Check the Top Roster Bar
1. At the top header, verify your **Classroom** (e.g., *LKG Jnana*, *LKG Satya*, *Nursery Jnana*) and **Observation Date**.
2. Below the header is the **Horizontal Student Roster Bar**:
   - **Roster Progress Counter:** Shows how many children are completed out of the total class (e.g., `28 / 28 — 100%`).
   - **Student Status Icons:**
     - 🟢 **Green Checkmark (`✓`):** Completed for today.
     - 🔴 **Red Cross (`✕`):** Marked Absent today (also counts as completed).
     - ⚪ **Gray Dot:** Pending (observation not yet saved for today).

### Step 3.2: Mark Attendance (`Present` or `Absent`)
On the active child's card:
- **If the child is Present:** Leave the toggle on **`✓ Present`** (green).
- **If the child is Absent:** Tap **`✕ Absent`** (red).
  - *Note:* When marked Absent, the 7 observation questions are automatically hidden. Simply tap **"Save & Next Child"** and the system records the absence and moves to the next child immediately.

### Step 3.3: Fill the 7 Core Observation Areas (For Present Children)
For a present child, tap the options that best describe their day:
1. **Engagement / Attention** *(Single choice)*: e.g., *Fully engaged*, *Mostly engaged*, *Partly engaged*.
2. **Participation** *(Single choice)*: e.g., *Initiated*, *Joined voluntarily*, *Joined when prompted*.
3. **Following / Response** *(Single choice)*: e.g., *Followed independently*, *Followed after prompting*.
4. **Thinking / Exploring** *(Multi-select — can tap multiple)*: e.g., *Asked questions*, *Tried independently*.
5. **Social / Communication** *(Multi-select)*: e.g., *Shared / cooperated*, *Interacted with other children*.
6. **State / Disposition Today (Mood)** *(Multi-select)*: e.g., *Happy / enthusiastic*, *Calm / comfortable*.
7. **Interest Today** *(Single choice + optional detail)*: e.g., *Noticeably greater interest* -> type what they enjoyed (e.g., *"Puzzles & blocks"*).
8. **Additional Observation / Quick Notes:** Tap any quick chip (e.g., *"Ate all lunch & snacks"*, *"Shared toys kindly with peers"*) or type your own custom note.

### Step 3.4: Saving & Moving to the Next Child
At the bottom of the screen, you have two save buttons:
- **💾 `Save`:** Saves the current child's record and stays on the same child's screen.
- **⏭️ `Save & Next` (Recommended):**
  1. Immediately saves the child's observation locally and uploads it to the cloud database.
  2. Turns the child's badge in the top roster bar to **🟢 Completed (`✓`)**.
  3. Automatically loads the **next child** in the class roll list so you can complete the entire class smoothly in minutes.

## 4. What Happens After Entering Student Data? (`Daily Summary` Tab)

Once you finish entering observations for your students, tap the **📊 `Daily Summary`** tab at the bottom. Here is everything you can do after data entry:

### 4.1: Verify 100% Class Completion
At the top of **Daily Summary**, you will see **4 clickable summary cards**:
- **Total Roster:** Total children in the classroom (e.g., `28`).
- **Completed:** Children whose observations are finished today.
- **Pending:** Children who still need their observation recorded today.
  - *Tip:* Tap the **Pending** card to filter the list to only unfinished children. Tap **`Observe →`** next to any pending child to jump straight to their form and finish it!
- **Absent:** Children marked absent today.

### 4.2: Actions Available for Each Completed Child
Next to every completed student in the list, you have **3 action buttons**:
1. **📲 Share to WhatsApp (Green Share Icon):**  
   Automatically formats a clean, emoji-styled Daily Observation Report for that child and opens WhatsApp so you can send it to parents with one tap.
2. **📄 Download PDF (Download Icon):**  
   Generates an official, printable A4 **Daily Child Observation Report PDF** with the school header, child details, all 7 observation domains, and a teacher signature line.
3. **✏️ Edit (`Edit →` Button):**  
   Re-opens that child's observation form if you want to update or add more notes for that day.

### 4.3: Reviewing Past Dates & Exporting Spreadsheets
- Use the **Calendar Date Picker** (or `<` / `>` arrows) at the top of `Daily Summary` to view records from any previous date.
- Tap **`Export CSV`** to download an Excel-ready spreadsheet of all observations for the selected date.

## 5. Student Onboarding & Class Roster Management (`Students` Tab)

Tap the **👥 `Students`** tab to view, filter, or onboard children.

### 5.1: Filtering Students by Classroom
- Use the horizontal **Classroom Filter Pills** (`All Classes`, `Playhome`, `Nursery Jnana`, `Nursery Shourya`, `LKG Jnana`, `LKG Satya`, `UKG Jnana`, `UKG Satya`, `UKG Shourya`) to view students in a specific section along with their exact headcount.

### 5.2: Enrolling a Single Student (`+ Add Child`)
1. Tap the **`+ Add Child`** button at the top right.
2. Enter the **Child's Full Name** (e.g., *Aarav Sharma*).
3. Select the **Classroom Section** from the dropdown (e.g., *LKG Jnana*).
4. *(Optional)* Add any **Special Notes** (allergies, parent contact note, etc.).
5. Tap **`Enroll Student`**.
   - **What Happens Automatically:**
     - The student is saved locally **and** immediately uploaded to the Cloud Database.
     - Your active classroom view automatically switches to that child's class so you see the newly added student immediately in the list!

### 5.3: Bulk Uploading Students (`Import Excel / CSV`)
1. Tap **`Import Excel / CSV`**.
2. Tap **`Download Sample Template`** if you need the standard column format (`Student Name`, `Class / Section`, `Notes`).
3. Select your `.xlsx` or `.csv` file and tap **Import**. All students are automatically matched to their classrooms and synced to the cloud.

## 6. AI-Based Progress Insights & Parent Reports (`Parent Slips` Tab)

Tap the **✨ `Parent Slips`** tab to generate automated multi-paragraph developmental reports and view a child's complete historical timeline.

### 6.1: How to Generate AI Progress Summaries
1. **Filter by Classroom** using the top pills (or leave on `All`), then **Select the Child** from the dropdown menu.
2. In the dark indigo **AI-Based Developmental & Learning Insights** card:
   - Choose the **Analysis Period** from the dropdown:
     - `Last 7 Recorded Days` *(Weekly Digest)*
     - `Last 30 Recorded Days` *(Monthly Report)*
     - `All Recorded Days` *(Full Term Progress)*
3. Tap the gold **`✨ Generate AI Progress Summary`** button.
4. Within seconds, the app analyzes all daily teacher uploads for that child and generates:
   - **★ Key Strength Badges:** e.g., *Attentive Learner*, *Active Participant*, *Positive Disposition*.
   - **📝 Overall Progress Summary:** A warm executive paragraph summarizing attendance and classroom growth.
   - **📚 Academic & Learning Patterns:** Paragraph analyzing attention span, engagement, instruction-following, and curiosity.
   - **🤝 Social & Participation:** Paragraph highlighting group participation, peer sharing, and communication.
   - **😊 Mood & Emotional State:** Paragraph describing daily emotional disposition, comfort level, and special interests.
   - **💡 Gentle Focus Areas & Recommendations:** 2–3 actionable, encouraging tips for parents and teachers.

### 6.2: Sharing or Printing the AI Report
Once the AI summary is generated, use the buttons at the top-right of the AI card:
- **🟢 `WhatsApp`:** Formats the complete AI multi-paragraph report for instant sharing with parents on WhatsApp.
- **⚪ `Letterhead PDF`:** Downloads an official **Institutional Letterhead Progress Evaluation Report PDF** formatted like an authentic school notepad with the Pragathi Vidyalaya School crest, Challakere campus address, official phone numbers, dual gold-navy pinstripes, student profile memo card, developmental domain tables, official teacher/principal signature blocks, seal placeholder, and developer attribution.
- **📋 `Copy`:** Copies the formatted summary text to your clipboard.

### 6.3: Viewing Individual Past Days
Scroll below the AI card to see the child's **Daily Observation History Timeline**, where you can view every past date recorded for that child and download or share individual daily slips.

## 7. Maintaining Daily Teacher Substitutions (`Daily Substitutes`)

When a regular classroom teacher is on leave or absent for the day, an **Administrator** can assign a **Substitute Teacher** so another teacher can record observations for that class.

### Step-by-Step: How to Assign a Substitute Teacher (Admin Only)
1. Tap your name / Menu (`▾`) in the top-right header and choose **`Daily Substitutes`** (or open **⚙️ Settings** -> **`Daily Substitutes`** tab).
2. **Select Date of Substitution:** Defaults to today's date (you can also pre-assign for an upcoming date).
3. **1. Class Needing Coverage (Absent Teacher):** Select the classroom whose regular teacher is absent (e.g., *LKG Jnana — Absent: Mrs. Kavitha*).
4. **2. Assign Substitute Teacher:** Select the teacher who will cover the class today from the staff dropdown.
5. **3. Reason / Notes (Optional):** e.g., *"Medical leave - morning session"*.
6. Tap **`Assign Substitute for [Date]`**.

### What Happens After Assigning a Substitute?
- **For the Substitute Teacher:** When the substitute teacher opens the app (or taps the 🔄 Sync icon), the delegated classroom automatically appears in their **Top Classroom Dropdown** for that date, along with an **amber banner** showing:  
  *`"Substitute Assignment Active: [Teacher Name] is covering this class today"`*.
- **To Remove a Substitution:** If a substitution was added by mistake, open `Settings → Daily Substitutes` and tap the **🗑️ Trash Icon** next to the active delegation.

## 8. Managing Staff Directory & School Holidays (`Settings`)

Administrators can tap their name / Menu (`▾`) in the top-right header (or open **⚙️ Settings**) to manage school-wide operations:

### 8.1: Staff Directory (`Staff Directory` Tab)
- **Add New Teacher / Admin:**
  1. Tap **`+ Add New Staff`**.
  2. Enter their **Full Name**, **Google Email Address** (must match the email they use to sign in), and **Role** (`Teacher` or `Admin`).
  3. **Assign Classrooms:** Tap the classroom pills they teach (or select *All Classes* for Admins/Floaters).
  4. Tap **`Save Staff Member`**.
- **Edit or Remove Staff:** Tap the **✏️ Edit** icon to change a teacher's assigned classes, or the **🗑️ Trash** icon to remove access.

### 8.2: Holidays & Closures (`Holidays & Closures` Tab — Supports Multi-Day Ranges!)
- **Mark a Single Day or Date Range Holiday:**
  1. **Start Date (From):** Select the first day of the holiday (e.g., *2026-10-10*).
  2. **End Date (To):** Select the final day of the vacation (e.g., *2026-10-18* for a 9-day Dussehra Vacation). For a single day, leave it the same as the start date.
  3. Notice the live helper indicator showing the exact number of vacation days selected (e.g., `🌴 Multi-Day Vacation Selected: 9 Days (2026-10-10 to 2026-10-18)`).
  4. Enter the **Holiday / Event Name** (e.g., *Dussehra Holidays*, *Deepavali Festival*).
  5. Select the **Category** (*Festival*, *Staff Training*, *School Event*, *Emergency Closure*, or *Other*).
  6. Tap **`Mark as School Holiday`**.
- **Effect of Marking a Holiday or Date Range:** On any date falling within that range, a rose/amber **School Holiday Notice Banner** appears across the top header, the `Observe` form, and the `Daily Summary` list displaying the holiday name, category, and date span. Observations are optional for those dates. *(Note: Sundays are also automatically detected as weekly holidays).*
- **Deleting a Multi-Day Holiday:** In the holiday list, multi-day vacations are highlighted with a `🌴 Multi-Day Range` badge. Tapping the trash icon removes the entire vacation range in one click!

### 8.3: Data Safety, Backups & Cloud Sync (`Cloud & System` Tab)
- **🔄 Top Header Sync Button:** Tap the cloud refresh icon in the top header anytime to pull the latest observations from all teacher devices.
- **Download Full Backup (JSON):** Saves a complete offline copy of all classrooms, students, and historical observations to your device.

## 9. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Why was a child showing as "Pending" even after a teacher entered notes?
- **Answer:** Previously, if a teacher typed an additional note or selected checkboxes without choosing **Question 1 (Engagement)**, the progress counter treated the record as incomplete. **This has now been fixed:** any present record with observation notes or checkboxes is automatically marked **Completed (`✓ Done`)**.

### Q2: I added a new student in the `Students` tab. Where do I see them?
- **Answer:** As soon as you click **`Enroll Student`**, the student is saved to the cloud and the app automatically switches your active classroom filter to that student's class so they appear right away in both the **`Students`** list and the **`Observe`** roster bar.

### Q3: Does the app work if classroom Wi-Fi / Mobile Data drops temporarily?
- **Answer:** **Yes!** The app uses an **Offline-First database** on your phone/tablet. Observations save instantaneously to your device even without internet, and automatically sync to the cloud as soon as your connection returns (or when you tap the 🔄 Sync button).

### Q4: How often can teachers generate AI Progress Summaries?
- **Answer:** Anytime! Once an AI summary is generated for a child, it is saved in the cloud so viewing it again, downloading the PDF, or sharing on WhatsApp is instant. Whenever new daily observations are added, simply tap **`Regenerate Summary`** to refresh the analysis with the latest days.
